Dungeon Tracker turns your D&D Beyond character into a quick play sheet for game night. Every roll is one tap away, with the numbers already worked out. HP, spell slots and class uses are tracked as you play. It only reads your sheet, so your D&D Beyond character stays exactly as it is.

Roll in style

Pick your dice

Every roll in your sheet is thrown with real-looking 3D dice. Choose from 24 sets: gold doubloons, carved wood, sea glass, gems, even dragonfire. Swap them any time from the sheet's menu.

How it works

  1. I

    Make your character public

    On D&D Beyond: open your character, then Character Settings → Character Privacy, and pick Public. Dungeon Tracker only reads it.

  2. II

    Paste the link

    Sign in with your email, no password needed, and paste your character's link.

  3. III

    Play

    Open it on your phone at the table. When you level up on D&D Beyond, pull the new version in with one tap.
