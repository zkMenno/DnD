How it works

🔓 Dungeon Tracker only works with public D&D Beyond characters. It reads the same sheet anyone with the link can see, so set your character to Public first (step I).

  1. I

    Make your character public

    • Open your character on D&D Beyond
    • Go to Character Settings → Character Privacy
    • Pick Public

    Your character can now be imported into Dungeon Tracker (read-only).

  2. II

    Paste the link

    Sign in with your email, no password needed.

  3. III

    Play

    Open it on your phone at the table. When you level up on D&D Beyond, pull the new version in with one tap.
