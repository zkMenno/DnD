Privacy & terms

In short. This is a free, unofficial hobby app for tracking D&D characters at the table. It stores only what it needs to work: your email, a username, your trackers and your notes. Everything is kept on a server in Germany (Hetzner), inside the EU, under European data protection law (GDPR). No ads, no tracking, no analytics, nothing sold or shared. You can download or delete your data yourself at any time on the home page.

Legal basis

Providing the app you signed up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and a legitimate interest in keeping it secure for the request logs (Art. 6(1)(f)).

What we never do

No advertising, no tracking or analytics tools, no profiling, no selling or sharing of data. Your trackers and notes are private unless you share a sheet with your party or with everyone signed in; shared sheets are read-only for others.

Where your data lives, and who else touches it
  • Hosting: Hetzner Online GmbH (Gunzenhausen, Germany), in its data centre in Falkenstein, Germany. All app data and backups stay there, inside the EU. Hetzner's data centres are ISO/IEC 27001 certified and Hetzner acts as a data processor bound by the GDPR.
  • Email: sign-in codes are sent through {{emailProvider}}, which only receives your email address and the message, to deliver it.
  • D&D Beyond: the server reads public character sheets from D&D Beyond by their character number. Nothing about you is sent to D&D Beyond, and this app never changes anything there.
  • Character portraits: a character's portrait picture, when it has one on D&D Beyond, is loaded by your browser straight from D&D Beyond (www.dndbeyond.com), as on their own site. D&D Beyond then sees your IP address, as with any picture on the web; this app sends them nothing else (no link to the page you're on).
  • Nothing else. Fonts, scripts and all other images are served from this server itself, so your browser doesn't contact Google or any other third party while you use the app.

App data is not transferred outside the European Union. Only the portrait pictures come from D&D Beyond, which may serve them from outside the EU.

Cookies and storage on your device

The app uses no cookies. It keeps a few things in your browser's own storage so it works quickly and offline: your sign-in, your last-opened sheets and unsent notes, and view settings like your dice set. These are strictly necessary for the app to work, so no consent banner is needed; nothing is used for tracking. Signing out clears your sheets and notes from that device.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can see, correct, download, and delete your data, object to how it's used, and limit it.

  • Download your data and delete your account yourself on the home page (Your data). Deleting removes your account, characters, trackers and notes straight away; they leave the backups within 15 days.
  • For anything else, email {{contact}}. You'll get an answer within a month.
  • You can also complain to a data protection authority, in France the CNIL (cnil.fr), or the one where you live.

To use the app you need to be old enough to agree to this under your country's law (15 in France, 16 in many EU countries), or have permission from a parent.

Terms of use
  • The app is free and offered as is, by a hobbyist: no guarantee it's always available, error-free, or that data is never lost. Keep your real character on D&D Beyond; this app is a table companion.
  • Only add characters you're allowed to look at (public D&D Beyond sheets), and verify only your own.
  • Be decent in party logs. Don't use the app to harass anyone, to break the law, or to attack or overload the service. Accounts that do can be removed.
  • You keep everything you write. You let the app store and show it to the people you share it with, nothing more.
